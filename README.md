# Global Superstore Sales Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. It uses the Global Superstore dataset to analyze business performance across different geographical regions, countries, states, cities, and shipping methods.

The dashboard helps understand sales distribution, profitability, order quantities, and shipping preferences through interactive visualizations.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- Data Visualization

## Dashboard Features

- **Sales and Profit Analysis:** Track overall sales and profit performance.
- **Discount and Quantity Analysis:** Monitor discounts offered and quantities sold.
- **Geographical Analysis:** Explore sales performance across countries, markets, states, and cities.
- **Regional Sales Analysis:** Compare sales across different geographical regions.
- **Shipping Mode Analysis:** Understand order distribution across different shipping methods.
- **Interactive Filters:** Filter the dashboard by country, region, market, and order date.

## Key Business Insights

The dashboard provides insights into the following business areas:

1. **Geographical Sales Performance:** Identifies high-performing countries, states, and cities based on sales, helping businesses understand where their revenue is generated.

2. **Regional Comparison:** Compares sales across different regions to identify stronger and weaker geographical markets.

3. **Market Analysis:** Visualizes country-level sales across different markets to understand geographical sales distribution.

4. **Shipping Preferences:** Analyzes the number of orders handled by different shipping modes, helping understand customer shipping preferences.

5. **Profitability Overview:** Provides an overview of total sales and profit to evaluate overall business performance.

6. **Discount and Quantity Analysis:** Tracks discount levels and quantities to support better pricing and inventory decisions.

## Business Applications

- Identify important geographical markets.
- Compare regional sales performance.
- Understand customer shipping preferences.
- Monitor overall profitability.
- Support better pricing and business decisions.
